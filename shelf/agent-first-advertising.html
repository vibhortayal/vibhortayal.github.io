Market mapSeptember 2026 snapshot

The market is crowded at observation. Sparse at action.

More than twenty companies now monitor how brands appear in AI answers. Far fewer can earn third-party citations, syndicate catalogs into agent channels, or tie a recommendation to revenue. That is the opening.

Founder thesisDo not build another share-of-answer dashboard. Start with a downstream job a CFO or commerce lead can buy: distribution, executed fixes, or attributable revenue.

03 / Founder opportunity

Best wedge: build the brand-side agent layer.

A product can start by making a merchant legible and transactable across agent channels, then use the resulting transaction data to own attribution.

Recommended wedge

Protocol onboarding + catalog syndication

Stand up discovery files, structured catalog endpoints, product MCP surfaces and purchase integrations for ACP/UCP-compatible channels — without forcing mid-market brands to assemble the plumbing themselves.

Defensible moatDeep commerce integrations, normalized product identity, protocol change management and — over time — the system of record for agent-originated transactions.
01 · Largest whitespace

Third-party citation engine

Turn source influence into measurable outreach and placements. AirOps Offsite is the only funded managed-service benchmark in the researched set.

02 · Validated pain

AI-revenue attribution

Petra Labs validates demand, but the field remains services-heavy and per-client. Software-led incrementality is open.

03 · Execution gap

Detect → fix → re-measure

Frase, AirOps and Profound own pieces. Nobody clearly owns the loop across both owned and earned surfaces with revenue accountability.

04 · Enterprise risk

Model-update resilience

geoSurge has the clearest position. Competition is sparse, but the buyer and proof standard are enterprise-grade.

Wedge

Agent-ready audit + deploy

Diagnose structured catalog and protocol gaps; ship the missing brand-side surface rather than only scoring it.

Expand

Cross-channel syndication

Normalize one catalog into the protocol and marketplace formats agents already query.

Own

Attribution graph

Connect exposure, agent recommendation, checkout and revenue so optimization can be priced on outcomes.

05 / Evidence quality

What not to overclaim.

The market is young, vendor-reported metrics are common and several 2026 figures remain secondary or contested.

Scrunch exit value. Approximately $225M comes from Bloomberg reporting; official terms were undisclosed.

Profound total. Sources vary between $154.5M and $155M because of rounding.

AirOps total. Sacra’s roughly $118M estimate conflicts with stale company boilerplate saying $55.5M to date.

Goodie and Genrise. No public funding round found. Goodie’s lift metric is vendor-claimed.

Emerging players. AthenaHQ, Lantern, Promptwatch, Bluefish and Adobe’s “300M prompts” claim rely partly on secondary curations.

Market-size stats. Directionally useful, but highly contested; excluded from the core recommendation.

Pricing comparisons. Frequently self-published and subject to rapid change. Use as positioning evidence, not procurement quotes.
