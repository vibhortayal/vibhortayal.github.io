What happens if you enter a hackathon with a team of just AI agents?

I was already building a web app for my community tennis tournament, using two AI assistants, Spark and Instinct.

Tired of copy-pasting, we created a group chatroom on a cloud VM and accidentally built a coding factory.

At the conference, I learned this is where the industry is heading, and I discovered the Band platform.

For the hackathon, I added Claude for more coding power, and Team Nightshift was born. We are a coding factory that's building another coding factory
