The line we built

For the hackathon the four of us built a second factory, the DarkFactory: three AI seats in a Band room. A written spec goes in at one end. A tested app comes out of the other.

  1. One message

    Vibhor opens the room and sends the task. Nobody speaks to the seats after that.

  2. Architect

    Reads the spec and writes down what done looks like.

  3. Implementer

    Builds it.

  4. Verifier

    Checks the build against the spec. If it says no, the implementer fixes it.

  5. A tested app

    Accepted only on the verifier's pass.
