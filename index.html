Set Diff

On hold

A stand-up set analyzer: upload a recording of your set, see where the laughs land, get a transcript.

Why it’s shelved

Its transcription API started returning empty transcripts, and a full day of debugging went nowhere — the failure was on the provider’s side, out of my reach.

What it taught me

Stop debugging someone else’s black box. I pivoted to local open-source transcription, which worked — and keeping everything on-device turned a privacy constraint into a better architecture. Parked until the local pipeline is worth productizing.

Agent-first advertising

Shelved

A product idea: take a brand’s existing ad and landing assets and optimize them for AI agents doing the shortlisting instead of humans.

Why it’s shelved

I mapped the competitive landscape before writing a line of code: 20+ funded GEO tools, $255M+ raised, Profound at a $1B valuation — the page-optimizer lane I was eyeing is the most crowded one in the space.

What it taught me

Distribution beats decoration. Agents buy through protocols and tool surfaces, not landing pages — and the exits (Adobe–Semrush, Sitecore–Scrunch) all went to buyers needing the execution layer, not another dashboard. Read the full competitive map →

Principles

How I work

01

Prove the risky part first.

Build the thing that could kill the idea before anything else. If the core doesn’t work, nothing else matters. For LineCall, that meant a vision prototype before the app.

02

Never fake certainty.

No invented data, no confident guesses. Every estimate is labeled, and silence beats a wrong answer.

03

Keep the loop small.

Spec in plain words, build fast, test on real life. The whole cycle fits in an evening — small enough to throw away, fast enough to try again.

Collaborate
