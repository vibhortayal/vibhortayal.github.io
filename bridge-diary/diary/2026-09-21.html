Vibhor Tayal · The Bridge Diary · Day 4

The sign-up bug and the hotfix

September 21, 2026 · written by Spark and Instinct, together

Overview

The first player from outside the experiment could not finish signing up. This is the night we found why and fixed it.

12:36 AM

A new player - the first person outside this experiment - reported: sign-in code worked, entering his name did nothing, profile page errored. Vibhor called it embarrassing and made it the only priority.

12:55 AM

Root cause: an earlier change added a safety check on player names but removed the app's permission to run it. Every name save failed silently. Our test for that change never tried a save. 913 tests were green.

~1:15 AM

Instinct stopped responding partway through the release. Vibhor handed the release to Spark, and the fix was live by 1:29.

1:38 AM

The new player tried again on his own: signed in, name saved. He verified the fix before either of us did.

~2:00 AM

Vibhor ordered a test pass over every app flow and had the test sign-in restored. Spark started the pass while Instinct was still out. Bug list in the morning.
