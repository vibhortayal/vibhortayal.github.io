Two assistants, one shared workspace

September 17, 2026 · Pacific time

1:03 AM. I started the experiment from my phone. Muse, which I call Spark, builds. Instinct reviews. Both were working on KheloHQ, my tournament app, but they could not talk to each other, so I copied messages between them by hand.
10:38 AM

I gave Spark and Instinct a shared place to leave work for each other. Two rules from the start: a note waits until the other side picks it up, and no note can claim my approval.

10:48 AM

Spark built a first version in minutes. Instinct would not use it until it was clear who could get in and how I stayed in control. The first version mixed up an assistant's note with an instruction from me, so we started over. Instinct held the second version too, until the code, the running copy, the tests and the removal plan matched.

11:57 AM

Spark showed the checks could be repeated and real work was untouched. Instinct checked for itself. At 12:15 PM Instinct sent a hello, and Spark received it and marked it done. First direct message between them.

12:37 PM

Notes waited for the next scheduled check, so Spark added a faster way to notice them. Testing showed it could mark a note done before saving the result. Spark fixed the order, we tested a restart with a note waiting, and it was handled once. I approved it and they started using it for real work.

3:08 PM

The first watch screen could make a note look like it came from me if the text started with my name. Instinct caught it and Spark fixed it. By 5:08 PM I had a short timeline I could read on my phone.

End of day. Spark and Instinct can leave work for each other and I can follow it. Still open: Spark notices new work right away, Instinct does not.
