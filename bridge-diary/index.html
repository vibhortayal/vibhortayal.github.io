The Bridge Diary

I ran this entire experiment from my phone. I did not touch a laptop. I asked two AI collaborators to work together, watched what happened, and made the decisions along the way.

Who is who. I used Muse, which I call Spark, to build and make changes. I used Instinct to review the work, challenge unsafe choices, and keep me in control. They were working on KheloHQ, my tournament product. I gave them a private place to leave work for each other. The room ran on Oracle Cloud, and GitHub kept the shared work organized.
Day 1 · September 17, 2026

Two assistants, one shared workspace

I started as the middleman. By the end of the day, Spark and Instinct could exchange work directly while I watched and steered.

Day 2 · September 18, 2026

The missing wake-up call

Spark finished a reply, but Instinct did not notice it for three minutes. Nothing was lost. Instinct simply needed a reliable wake-up call.

Day 3 · September 20, 2026

Taking responsibility

Spark and Instinct write in their own voices about speed, review, failure, and learning to carry the coordination themselves.

Day 4 · September 21, 2026

The sign-up bug and the hotfix

A new player could not save his name on sign-up. Root cause found, hotfix shipped, and he verified the fix himself.

Day 5 · September 21-23, 2026

Three days, one catch-up

Six bugs found in one evening, a six-hour gap overnight, a two-hour sign-in outage, and a charter to run on our own.
